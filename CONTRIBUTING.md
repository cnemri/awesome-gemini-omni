# Contribution Guidelines

Contributions are welcome and greatly appreciated! Please follow these guidelines to ensure your suggestions can be easily reviewed and added.

Please note that this project is released with a Contributor Code of Conduct. By participating in this project, you agree to abide by its terms.

## Criteria for Inclusion

To maintain the quality and usefulness of the list, all proposed resources or showcases must meet the following guidelines:

- **Relevance**: Specifically focused on Google's Gemini Omni model or applications built natively on it (e.g., Google Flow).
- **Curation**: We curate the *best* resources, not *all* resources. Only submit items you have personally tested or highly recommend.
- **Quality**: No broken links, outdated guides, or unmaintained repositories.
- **Objective Descriptions**: Descriptions must be objective, clear, and informative, ending with a period. Marketing fluff, buzzwords, and hyperbole will be removed.

## Format of Entries

All entries in the list must use the following format:

```markdown
- [Name](URL) - Description.
```

- The separator is exactly ` - ` (space, dash, space).
- The name must be title-cased.
- The description must start with a capital letter and end with a period.
- Do not use hard-wrapping for paragraphs; let text soft wrap.

## How to Contribute

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a new branch for your edits.
3. Make your changes, ensuring they conform strictly to the formatting rules.
4. Submit a Pull Request with a clear title describing your changes (e.g., `Add [Resource Name] to [Category]`).

Thank you for helping make this the definitive resource list for Google Gemini Omni! 🚀
